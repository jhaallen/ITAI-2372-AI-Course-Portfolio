# AI in Government Services

## Project Overview

This project explores how artificial intelligence can improve access to government and public services. The proposed system would allow residents to explain their needs in everyday language and receive clearer guidance about relevant programs, services, and next steps.

## Key Areas Explored

- AI-supported public service navigation
- Improving access to government information
- Natural language communication
- Responsible use of resident data
- Human oversight and system accountability
- Expanding successful public-sector AI practices across agencies

## Skills Demonstrated

- Public-sector AI strategy
- Human-centered solution design
- Ethical AI analysis
- Research and case-study evaluation
- Accessibility and service improvement
- Written communication
