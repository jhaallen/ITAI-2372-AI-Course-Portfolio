# AI in Manufacturing

## Project Overview

This project examines how artificial intelligence is transforming manufacturing through smarter production systems, predictive insights, robotics, and greater coordination between people, machines, and operational data.

It also explores a proposed AI-supported manufacturing platform that could connect systems such as manufacturing execution software, robotics, and CNC equipment to improve efficiency, visibility, and decision-making across production environments.

## Key Areas Explored

- Predictive maintenance and equipment monitoring
- AI-supported quality control
- Robotics and manufacturing automation
- Manufacturing execution systems
- CNC equipment integration
- Workforce training and job transformation
- Responsible implementation and human oversight

## Skills Demonstrated

- Manufacturing AI research
- Operational problem-solving
- AI solution design
- Systems-thinking
- Ethical and workforce impact analysis
- Technology implementation planning
- Written communication
