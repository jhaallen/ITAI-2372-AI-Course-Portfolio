# ITAI 2372 Artificial Intelligence Course Portfolio

**Jha Allen**

AI strategist and trainer with experience helping organizations understand, adopt, and apply artificial intelligence to workforce development, business operations, and organizational challenges.

## Portfolio Overview

This portfolio highlights coursework, research, and applied projects completed during ITAI 2372. The course strengthened my ability to evaluate artificial intelligence across industries, identify practical use cases, consider ethical and workforce implications, and design responsible AI-supported solutions.

## Featured Projects

### [Workforce Intelligence System](01-Featured-Projects/01-Workforce-Intelligence-System)

A proposed AI-supported system designed to help employers, educational institutions, and workforce development organizations identify skills gaps and better align training with labor-market needs.

### [AI in Government Services](01-Featured-Projects/02-AI-in-Government-Services)

An exploration of how AI can help residents navigate government programs and public services through clearer, more accessible, everyday communication.

### [AI in Manufacturing](01-Featured-Projects/03-AI-in-Manufacturing)

An examination of how AI, robotics, operational data, and connected manufacturing systems can improve production, maintenance, quality control, and decision-making.
