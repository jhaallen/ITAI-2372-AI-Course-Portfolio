# Course Assignments

This section contains additional assignments completed throughout ITAI 2372. These projects examine how artificial intelligence is being applied across industries while considering implementation, ethics, workforce impact, and future opportunities.

## Included Assignments

### AI in Retail
Explores how retailers use artificial intelligence for personalization, inventory management, customer service, and operational decision-making.

### AI and Blockchain
Examines how artificial intelligence and blockchain can work together to improve security, transparency, automation, and data-driven decision-making.

### AI in Media and Entertainment
Reviews the use of artificial intelligence in content recommendation, production, audience engagement, and personalized entertainment experiences.

### AI in Fraud Detection
Analyzes how financial organizations use artificial intelligence to identify suspicious activity, reduce fraud, and protect customers and payment systems.

### Ethics of Social Media Recommendation Algorithms
Evaluates the benefits and ethical concerns associated with recommendation systems, including privacy, bias, transparency, user influence, and misinformation.

## Skills Strengthened

- Industry research and analysis
- Responsible AI evaluation
- Ethical decision-making
- Identification of AI use cases
- Written communication
- Technology and workforce impact analysis
