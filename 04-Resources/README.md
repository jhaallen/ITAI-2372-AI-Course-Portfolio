# AI and Workforce Resources

This section includes selected resources that supported my research and understanding throughout ITAI 2372. These materials provided guidance on responsible AI, workforce transformation, labor-market information, ethics, and practical AI implementation.

## Responsible AI and Governance

### NIST AI Risk Management Framework
A framework created to help organizations identify, evaluate, and manage risks associated with artificial intelligence systems.

https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

### OECD AI Principles
International principles focused on trustworthy, transparent, accountable, and human-centered artificial intelligence.

https://oecd.ai/en/ai-principles

### UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of Artificial Intelligence
Guidance addressing human rights, fairness, transparency, data governance, education, and the broader social impact of AI.

https://www.unesco.org/en/artificial-intelligence/recommendation-ethics

## Workforce and Labor-Market Resources

### O*NET Online
A career and workforce database containing information about occupations, required skills, work activities, and job characteristics.

https://www.onetonline.org/

### U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Occupational Outlook Handbook
A resource offering information on occupations, education requirements, wages, employment trends, and projected job growth.

https://www.bls.gov/ooh/

### World Economic Forum Future of Jobs Report 2025
A report examining how technology, artificial intelligence, economic changes, and skills gaps are affecting employers and the future workforce.

https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/digest/

## How These Resources Supported My Work

These resources helped support my analysis of:

- Responsible and ethical AI adoption
- Workforce skills gaps and changing job requirements
- Human oversight and accountability
- AI governance and risk management
- Workforce development and organizational planning
- The effect of artificial intelligence on jobs and industries
